# Norfolk Presbyterian polling-site labels

The [PCUSA congregation directory](https://pcusa.org/congregation/second-church-norfolk-va) identifies Second Presbyterian Church at 7305 Hampton Boulevard in Norfolk. Its [Third Presbyterian entry](https://pcusa.org/congregation/third-church-norfolk-va) identifies Third Presbyterian Church at 8065 Carlton Street. Precincts 106 and 510 use the display labels `Second Presbyterian Church, PCUSA` and `Third Presbyterian Church, PCUSA` respectively.

The county-and-code overrides in `scripts/build_precinct_friendly_names.js` preserve these labels when the friendly-name index is rebuilt.
